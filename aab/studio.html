Before it goes out
    Publish as files instead

    For when the database isn't there, or you want the piece in the repository rather than in D1. The index entry is the part that makes it appear on the Insights page, in Ctrl+K and in the feed — publishing to the database does all of that on its own.

    1. Press Publish to the site. Photos are uploaded to /media first, so the article itself stays small and the same photo pasted twice is only stored once.
    2. That's it: the piece is live, the Insights page picks it up, and readers can react and ask questions on it straight away.
    3. Changed your mind? Open… loads anything back in to edit and republish, and Published on the desk unpublishes with one click.
    1. Download the page: you get your-slug.html.
    2. Put that file in the insights/ folder of the site.
    3. Click Get the index entry, copy it, and paste it at the top of the ARTICLES list in content.js. That's what puts the card on the Insights page and the article in Ctrl+K search.
    4. Commit and push. That's the whole publish.
    Photos. Every one is resized to 1600px on its long side and re-encoded as WebP, which also strips the location data phones bury in JPEGs.

    Publishing uploads them to /media and leaves a link in the article, so the piece itself stays a few kilobytes and the same photo used twice is stored once.

    Downloading embeds them in the page instead, so one file is the whole article. That's simplest, but the page grows: if the meter goes gold or red, use the .zip, which gives you the page plus a photos/ folder already wired up. Unzip both into insights/.
