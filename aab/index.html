Open one and take it apart

Every model below runs in the browser. Change an assumption and watch the statements, the valuation or the charts move with it, no download, no sign-up.

01 Three-statement model: a DSE-listed manufacturer → Linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow with a revolver and a scenario switch. The balance check is on the page, and it ties. 02 DCF with two-way sensitivity tables → WACC built up from its parts rather than typed in, two terminal value methods that quote each other back, and a grid where every cell is a full revaluation. 03 Index volatility & drawdowns: Python → Rolling volatility, drawdown episodes with recovery times, fat tails against a normal curve, and what holding periods actually do. Bring your own CSV. 04 Islamic vs conventional funds: an MSc dissertation → 220 UK equity funds and 19,577 fund-months through five-factor and Carhart models. The hypothesis failed, and the power analysis shows why a sample of three could never have tested it. 05 Stock check: a full screening framework → Thirty-eight ratios across six pillars, weighted by investor style, with a separate ratio set for banks. Every score shows its own working, in English or Bangla.

Why this site exists

The jargon is the barrier

Most Bangladeshi savings decisions are made without the vocabulary to examine them, not from a lack of intelligence, but because the explanations only exist in English, written for people who already understand. That's a solvable problem.

Arithmetic before opinion

Whether sanchayapatra beats an FDR is not a matter of opinion; it's a calculation, and it changes with the tax at source and how long you can leave the money. So the calculators come with the writing.

Say what isn't known

Rates change, rules change, and automated news filters miss things. Every page here says what it assumes and where it could be wrong. Nothing on this site tells you what to buy.
